Corporate Profile

Connecting Indian Agriculture to International Markets

Kailash Global Impex is an India-based export company focused on connecting quality agricultural products with international markets. Headquartered in Visnagar, Gujarat, we operate with a commitment to disciplined sourcing, reliable supplier relationships, and buyer-focused fulfillment.

Our export operations specialize in Raw Tobacco Leaf and Moringa Leaf Powder, sourced from established agricultural belts across India and prepared to meet buyer-specific commercial requirements.
